Inclusion (manuscript Sec. IV-A, verbatim)

We include a paper if it states a formal, statistical or architectural guarantee about a learned component, or about a system containing one, and if the object its guarantee quantifies over can be identified. We exclude empirical attack studies without a guarantee, guarantees about non-learned components, and theses that duplicate a journal version.

Two tiers (verbatim)

The inclusion rule in our proposal required evaluation on an autonomous vehicle. Applied literally, it would exclude the canonical verifiers that define the guarantee classes, which are evaluated on image benchmarks. We therefore code two tiers. Tier A contains guarantees evaluated on a vehicle task, including standard vehicle benchmarks such as ACAS Xu, TaxiNet, adaptive cruise control and quadrotors. Tier B contains method anchors evaluated only on generic benchmarks. Both tiers are included.

Screening convention

A record that might meet the rule is included for full-text reading; uncertainty is resolved at full text, not here. A record with no abstract is judged on its title and the same convention.

Order and stopping (amendment 1, fixed 2026-10-15)

Records are shown best-first by a ranking model trained on human decisions; the model is retrained after every 200 consensus decisions. Screening stops only when at least 2,538 records (10%) have been screened by both of you and the last 250 consecutive records were excluded by both, followed by a model switch and a random check of 400 unscreened records. Screen independently: do not discuss records until adjudication.
