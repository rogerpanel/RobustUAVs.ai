% ------------------------------------------------------------- Related work
\paragraph{Stealthy and disguised attacks on control systems.}
Stealthy attacks have been studied from false-data injection against
$\chi^2$-monitored Kalman filters~\cite{mo2010fdi,mo2009replay} and the attack
space of Teixeira et al.~\cite{teixeira2015}, through impact limited by a
false-alarm budget~\cite{urbina2016} and reachable-set
metrics~\cite{mo2016,murguia2020}, to $\varepsilon$-stealthiness in KL
divergence~\cite{bai2017}, attacks that alarm at the false-alarm
rate~\cite{hashemi2018}, undetectable unknown inputs~\cite{pasqualetti2013},
interval observers under bounded noise~\cite{degue2022} and secure estimation
under sparse attacks~\cite{mishra2015}. Each treats the benign perturbation as a
single, known nuisance. Fault-mimicking actuator attacks~\cite{roy2023} and
separability analyses~\cite{xue2025dual,li2026unified} concern a fixed attack
against a fixed detector; equivalence concerns the attacker's best response,
and against every detector we built some small-amplitude disguise evaded
(\cref{sec:eval}), a question power grids share~\cite{borgeshink2014,ghosh2024,ahsan2026,sayed2024gridchaos}.

\paragraph{Attacks on UAV navigation.}
GNSS spoofing has advanced from portable spoofers and takeover
conditions~\cite{humphreys2008,tippenhauer2011} to covert UAV
capture~\cite{kerns2014}, adaptive hijacking~\cite{noh2019} and attacks on
multi-sensor fusion~\cite{shen2020,zeng2018}, surveyed in~\cite{psiaki2016,zeng2026survey}.
Stealthy attacks against control-based defences~\cite{dash2019,dash2021dtrap},
black-box GPS attacks~\cite{khazraei2023,khazraei2024}, optimal attacks on
nonlinear estimators~\cite{hallyburton2022}, sensor
reconfiguration~\cite{erba2025}, wind-exploiting
spoofers~\cite{kim2024predictable}, real-time redirection~\cite{meng2026phantom},
acoustic inertial injection~\cite{son2015,trippel2017} and the hardness of
physical sensor attacks~\cite{kim2024hardness} establish that stealth is
possible, but none treats the disguise as the attacker's optimisation variable
or links undetected deviation to a mission-level certificate.

\paragraph{Detection and recovery.}
Control and physical invariants~\cite{choi2018,quinonez2020savior}, software
sensors~\cite{choi2020raid}, PID-Piper~\cite{dash2021pidpiper}, diagnosis-guided
recovery~\cite{dash2024delorean}, safety-critical variable
monitoring~\cite{park2023scvmon}, PWM-fused IDSs~\cite{asif2024confide} and
inertial-attack recovery~\cite{meng2025mars} are the defences an attacker in our
model must evade; active exposure~\cite{tian2026} and
fuzzing~\cite{kim2019rvfuzzer,kim2021pgfuzz} complement them. Hardening learned intrusion detectors against
evasion~\cite{evasionids2024} or calibrating their false-alarm operating
point~\cite{hgpids2026} does not bound what an undetected attacker achieves. Power-distortion
and INS-aided GNSS monitors with integrity-risk bounds~\cite{wesson2018pdm,tanil2018}
are the closest analogue of our budget, without a fault or environment term or
unknown dependence.

\paragraph{Certificates and reachability.}
Randomized smoothing~\cite{cohen2019smoothing,salman2019,lecuyer2019} and its
reinforcement-learning extensions~\cite{kumar2022policy,wu2022crop,mu2024receps}
certify models or rewards, not missions. Set-based
reachability~\cite{althoff2021,althoff2010,scott2016}, tube model predictive
control~\cite{mayne2005}, funnels~\cite{tedrake2010,majumdar2017}, control
barrier functions~\cite{ames2019}, Hamilton--Jacobi
reachability~\cite{bansal2017} and Simplex~\cite{sha2001} supply envelopes, and
probabilistic zonotopes assume independent components~\cite{althoff2010,modares2026pz}.
The nearest prior work to our budget is stochastic reach-avoid games against an
adversary plus nature~\cite{ding2013games}, with a known kernel, and Wasserstein
distributionally robust reachability for stealthy injection under ambiguous
noise~\cite{feng2025wdr}, without dependence between components or a
mission-level floor; conformal methods~\cite{lindemann2023ral,lindemann2023iccps}
assume exchangeability, which an adaptive attacker breaks.

\paragraph{Dependence uncertainty.}
Sharp bounds on sums with fixed marginals~\cite{makarov1981,ruschendorf1982,frank1987},
the Rearrangement Algorithm~\cite{puccetti2012,puccetti2013,embrechts2013var,hofert2020,bernard2026}
and probability-bounds analysis~\cite{williamson1990,ferson2003,ferson2004,bouissou2012}
are mature in finance, reliability~\cite{song2020dba} and power
systems~\cite{papaefthymiou2009,arrigo2023,amiri2021}, and appear in verification
of neural networks~\cite{goubault2024,pifarre2026,chwialkowski2026} and motion
planning~\cite{ilyes2023stori}. To our knowledge, no prior work applies them
across an adversarial, a stochastic and an environmental perturbation to certify
a cyber-physical mission outcome.
